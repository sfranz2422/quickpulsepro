Privacy policy

For QuickPulse Pro (quickpulsepro.onrender.com). Last updated October 2, 2026.

QuickPulse Pro is a classroom tool for live polls, review quizzes, flash cards and tests, run by Steve Franz, a teacher. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Students

Polls, quizzes and flash cards can be used without signing in. Your answers are stored so your teacher can see the class's results. Flash cards also store a random browser session ID with your answers. None of this is connected to your name unless you sign in.

Tests require signing in with Google. Signing in asks Google for your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. Nothing else from your Google account is requested. With them the site stores:

  • your name and email, so your teacher knows whose answers are whose;
  • your answers to tests, when you started and submitted, and the scores your teacher gives;
  • your answers to polls and quizzes you take while signed in, labelled with your name.

Only the teacher who made a poll, quiz or test, and the site's administrator, can see the answers to it.

Teachers

A teacher account stores a username, email address and password (stored hashed, never in readable form), along with the polls, quizzes, flash cards and tests the teacher creates.

Google Classroom (teachers only)

A teacher can choose to connect their Google Classroom so the site can list their classes, post tests to them as assignments, look up the students on those class rosters (names and email addresses, to match students to their test results), and send test grades back. Students are never asked for Classroom access. Only tests use Classroom; polls, quizzes and flash cards do not.

To do this the site keeps a Google access token for that teacher, encrypted. It is used only for those purposes, only when the teacher acts, and is deleted when they press Disconnect. They can also remove access at any time from their Google account's security settings. Class rosters are looked up when needed and not stored.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where it is kept, and who else is involved

  • The site and its database are hosted by Render, which keeps ordinary request logs (including IP addresses) for a short time.
  • Pages load styles and scripts from public content networks (such as jsDelivr) and Google's sign-in script, which see your IP address as any website would.
  • Cookies are used only to keep you signed in, to protect forms, and to remember answers you've already given.

Deleting your information

Teachers can delete their own polls, quizzes, tests and the answers to them at any time. To have a student or teacher account and everything attached to it deleted, email the address below.
